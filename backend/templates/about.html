1. Real Genomic Dataset Collection & Provenance

TCGA PanCancer Atlas via cBioPortal API

The predictive engine is trained exclusively on documented, real cancer genomic datasets from the TCGA PanCancer Atlas downloaded through the official cBioPortal API. No fabricated patients, synthetic mutations, or random cancer labels are ever used in training or evaluation.

2. Zero-Patient-Leakage Preprocessing

Methodological Rigor Guarantee

ONCOGENIX AI enforces a strict StratifiedGroupKFold on patient_id (80% Train: 1,942 samples; 20% Held-Out Test: 486 samples). All samples from a given patient remain exclusively in train or test, strictly preventing data contamination.

✓ Zero patient overlap between training and evaluation folds.
✓ Evaluates 40 verified pan-cancer driver gene features.
✓ Preserves authentic prevalence without distortion.
